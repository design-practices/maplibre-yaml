Two refinements to the story layout. The narrow side-card layout for a phone on its side now applies to touch screens only, so a short desktop browser window or an embedded frame keeps the `alignment` the story asks for. A legend or slot that moves out of a chapter card's way no longer lands on MapLibre's controls in another corner, on the map's notice, or on another corner's chrome.
