Story chapter cards no longer hide what the chapter is about. On wide screens a centre-aligned chapter (the default) put its card over the middle of the map, right on the chapter's `center`; the camera is now offset so that point shows above the card, as it already did beside left/right cards and on phones. On phones, a legend or slot in a bottom corner of a story map now moves up above the resting chapter card instead of sitting underneath it.
