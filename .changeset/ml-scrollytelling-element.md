Story maps now work without a build step: `@maplibre-yaml/core/register` (and the CDN bundle) defines `<ml-scrollytelling>`, which shows a `type: scrollytelling` document as a scroll-driven story in any HTML page. Give it the story as `src="story.yaml"`, an inline `<script type="text/yaml">`, or a `story` property; it renders a sticky `<ml-map>` (slots, legend and the map's own error handling included) with the chapter cards scrolling over it, and runs the chapters: camera moves, `layers.show`/`hide`, every chapter action, the marker rail, themes, alignment and the footer, dispatching `ml-scrollytelling:chapter`, `ml-scrollytelling:action` and a new `ml-scrollytelling:error`. Astro's `<Scrollytelling>` now renders this same element (same props, same classes and events), so a story behaves identically in an Astro site and a plain page. `@maplibre-yaml/astro` 0.6 therefore requires `@maplibre-yaml/core` 0.8 or later (its peer range is now `>=0.8.0`): upgrade both together. The engine is also importable on its own, DOM-free, from `@maplibre-yaml/core/scrollytelling`, with its stylesheet at `@maplibre-yaml/core/scrollytelling.css`. Fixed along the way, on both paths: the chapter rail's dots take mouse clicks (the chapter column used to cover them) and arrow keys; on phones the camera is offset so a chapter's subject shows above its card, and the rail no longer sits on the card; a `fitBounds` action frames top-down at the chapter's own pitch and bearing instead of keeping the previous chapter's tilt; under reduced motion every chapter camera move and camera action is instant on every supported maplibre-gl major; a `src` story no longer flashes `<ml-map>`'s "No configuration provided" while it loads; and a story that fails shows one error card, with line numbers and did-you-mean hints, instead of two. The story map's own pan/zoom gestures now start off (the wheel and touch scroll the story, which they effectively did before) so the rail, legend, markers and popups on the map are clickable; set `scrollZoom: true` or `dragPan: true` in the story's `config:` to turn one back on.
