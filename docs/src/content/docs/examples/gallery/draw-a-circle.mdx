Replicates the MapLibre example [Draw a circle](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/draw-a-circle/). The fill and outline layers and their shared source are YAML; the source starts empty. The polygon is computed at runtime from a centre and a radius — what `turf.circle` does upstream, in a few lines — and handed to the layers with `updateLayerData()`. A slider in a corner slot recomputes it.

## What to notice

- A circle that never changes needs no JS: precompute the polygon and paste it into `data:` — the page is then pure YAML.
- The two layers share one named source, so one `updateLayerData` call redraws both.
