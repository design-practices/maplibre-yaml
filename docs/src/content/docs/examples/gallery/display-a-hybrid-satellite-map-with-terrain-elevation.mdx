## What to notice

- Upstream edits the basemap inside a `transformStyle` callback. Each of its four edits is one declaration here: `projection:`, `terrain:`, `sky:`, and a raster layer with `before:` naming the basemap's first road layer, so roads and labels draw over the imagery.
- `sky.atmosphere-blend` is a zoom expression: the atmosphere is full at zoom 0 and gone by zoom 2.
- Globe and the atmosphere need **maplibre-gl 5.0.0** or later. On 4.x the page warns once and renders in mercator; terrain still works.
