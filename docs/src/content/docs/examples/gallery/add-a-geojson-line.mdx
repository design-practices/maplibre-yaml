## What to notice

- `route:` expands to a `Feature<LineString>` at parse time — the emitted `style.json` carries canonical GeoJSON, so nothing downstream knows sugar was involved.
- `layout` and `paint` keys are the MapLibre style-spec names, verbatim; no translation table to learn.
