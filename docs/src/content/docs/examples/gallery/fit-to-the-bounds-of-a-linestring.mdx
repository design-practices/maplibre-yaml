Replicates the MapLibre example [Fit to the bounds of a LineString](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/fit-to-the-bounds-of-a-linestring/). Upstream folds the line's coordinates into an `LngLatBounds` and calls `map.fitBounds` from a button. Here `config.fitTo` names the source, and the map opens already framed on the route. No coordinates are copied into the camera.

## What to notice

- `fitTo.source` names an entry in `sources:`, and `padding` and `maxZoom` mirror MapLibre's `fitBounds` options. The bounds come from the same walk the `zoomToFeature` click interaction uses, applied to the whole source.
- Inline `data:` is framed at construction, so the camera never jumps. A `url:` source opens at the authored `center`/`zoom` and is framed once its first fetch lands. Later refreshes don't re-fit.
- `center`/`zoom` stay required. They are the camera before the data arrives, and the fallback when the fit can't apply. Tiled sources (vector, raster) have no client-side extent and keep the authored camera, with a warning.
- Upstream fits on a button click. The button itself is page chrome, not something the format expresses. The initial-camera fit is the declarative half.
- **Eject class: ejects via fallback.** `mlym emit --with-fallbacks` computes the fitted `center`/`zoom` from the inline line for a 1024×768 reference viewport and writes them as the style's root camera. This is reported as lossy, because a live map fits its own container. `--strict` refuses.
