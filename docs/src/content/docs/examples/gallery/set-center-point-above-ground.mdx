Replicates the MapLibre example [Set center point above ground](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/set-center-point-above-ground/). The camera centres on a point 541 m up — the top of One World Trade Center — rather than on the ground beneath it. `elevation` and `centerClampedToGround` are ordinary MapLibre map options, so they pass straight through `config:`; the extruded buildings are a document layer.

## What to notice

- Upstream opens at an 85° pitch and lifts `maxPitch` to 105° to look up from below the horizon. The schema caps pitch at 85° (tracked as ml-rww), and on maplibre-gl 5.x an 85° view loads only low-detail building tiles, so this page opens at 70° — drag to tilt further.
- Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, via OpenFreeMap.
- `elevation` and `centerClampedToGround` need maplibre-gl ≥ 5.0; on 4.x they are ignored and the camera centres on the ground.
