## What to notice

- `config.bounds` (`[west, south, east, north]`) wins over `center`/`zoom` for the initial camera — the upstream version needs a `fitBounds()` call after construction.
- Fitting to a *source's* extent rather than literal bounds is a tracked gap (see the gallery index).
