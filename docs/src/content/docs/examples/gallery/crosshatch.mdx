A Mapzen classic. Lower Manhattan as a pen-and-ink drawing, after the
[crosshatch style](https://github.com/tangrams/tangram-sandbox/blob/gh-pages/styles/crosshatch.yaml)
Patricio Gonzalez Vivo wrote for Mapzen's Tangram in 2015. Ground, parks and
water use Tangram's own hatch and paper textures. Buildings are hatched
extrusions, and labels are set in a serif italic.

## The static tier

This page is the **static tier** of the classic. It is one YAML document,
plain style spec from top to bottom: no page JavaScript, no shader, no
runtime beyond MapLibre itself. As a result the document is its own eject:
`mlym emit --strict` compiles it with **zero warnings**. Nothing is
declared absent, nothing is lossy, and the emitted `style.json` renders the
same map in vanilla MapLibre.

What it reproduces exactly, and what it approximates:

- **Exact: ground, landuse and water.** Tangram draws these unlit: a
  hatch texture repeated per tile, mixed from ink to paper, and a
  crumpled-paper normal map under fixed lights. Each one bakes to a plain
  image, and a `*-pattern` repeats that image just as Tangram did.
- **Approximate: buildings.** In Tangram the buildings are the lit part,
  with stroke *density* following the light on each face. Here every face
  gets the same light pen hatch, and the document's `light:` shades the
  faces. Tangram's height exaggeration (×2.25 at z13 down to ×1 at z18) is
  kept, as one `interpolate`.

## What the effect adds

The tonal hatching on building faces is the job of the experimental
`tonal-hatch` [effect](/guides/effects/): a MapLibre custom layer that
keeps this document as its fallback and upgrades the `buildings` layer in
place. Where the effect isn't loaded, and in every ejected style, this
page's look is what renders. The [Mapzen classics](/classics/) page shows
both side by side.

## What to notice

- **`images:` + `*-pattern`.** Every texture is a declared image. On eject
  the textures are fetched into the document sprite and the references are
  rewritten to `mlym:<name>`. Their URLs are absolute, because emit fetches
  at compile time and has no page to resolve a relative path against.
- **A data-driven pattern.** `landcover` selects its texture with
  `["match", ["get", "class"], "sand", "earth", "landuse"]`. Both outputs
  are rewritten on eject, with no warning.
- **`light:`.** This is the style-spec light, anchored to the map so the
  shading stays put as the map turns, the way Tangram's world-fixed lights
  do. Change `position` or `intensity` to retune the contrast between faces.
  See [Light](/schema/map-config/#light).
- **Self-hosted glyphs.** The inline base style exists only to point
  `glyphs` at this site's Libre Baskerville Italic ranges. That is an
  [OFL](https://openfontlicense.org) font standing in for Tangram's
  Baskerville italic.
- **Reproducible textures.** `mlym bake crosshatch --out <dir>` regenerates
  all four PNGs from Tangram's MIT-licensed source textures. See
  [`bake`](/cli/reference/#bake).
